Notifence Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Notifence is an Android app for keeping a local history of notifications. Contact the developer about privacy at notifence@gruv.top.

What the app accesses

If you enable notification access in Android settings, Notifence receives notifications that Android delivers to its notification listener. These may contain app names and package identifiers, notification titles and text, senders or conversation names, timestamps, categories, and other notification metadata. Notification content can include personal or sensitive information. Notifence uses this information to create and search your history, recognize updates when possible, and show gaps when capture cannot be verified. You can exclude selected apps from future recording; this does not remove entries already saved.

Where information is stored and shared

Notification history is stored on your device in an encrypted SQLCipher database. The database secret is protected using Android Keystore. Notifence does not request Android's INTERNET permission and has no account, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising SDK, telemetry, or developer-operated backend. The app does not automatically send notification history or diagnostic information to the developer or other parties. The app's settings are stored locally.

If you choose to copy notification details or diagnostic information, Android's clipboard and any apps you choose to share it with may gain access to what you copied. Diagnostic information can include app and notification metadata and content fingerprints; do not share it unless you intend to. If you contact us by email, your email provider handles that communication under its own terms.

Security and retention

History is kept for 30 days by default. You can select another available retention period, including keeping history indefinitely. Older local records are removed according to your selected setting. You can stop future capture by revoking notification access in Android settings; this does not erase existing history. Removing Notifence normally removes its private local app data under Android's app-uninstall behavior. If encrypted storage becomes unreadable, a reset that you explicitly confirm removes the local encrypted history and creates an empty database; the old history cannot be recovered.

Optional App Lock protects access to the app's interface using device authentication. It does not lock the encryption key or stop background capture. Screenshot protection is enabled by default and can be changed in Settings. The app is configured to exclude its data from Android backup and device transfer, although behavior of system or manufacturer snapshots may vary.

Updates and questions

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current version will be available at this address. For privacy questions, contact notifence@gruv.top.
